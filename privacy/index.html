Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026

TikTok Bot ("the App") is a private tool used only by the owner of the TikTok account @justonlytariq to upload their own videos to TikTok as drafts.

Information we access

  • Basic TikTok profile information (open ID, display name, username) to confirm the correct account is connected.
  • An access token issued by TikTok, used only to upload videos to the account's TikTok inbox.
  • The video files the owner chooses to upload.

How it is used

This information is used only to upload the owner's videos as drafts. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising or tracking.

Storage

Tokens and credentials are stored securely and are never published. Data is kept only as long as needed to operate the App.

Your choices

You can revoke the App's access at any time in your TikTok settings under Security > Manage app permissions. On request, any stored data will be deleted.

Children

The App is not directed at anyone under 18.

Changes

This policy may be updated by posting a new version on this page.
